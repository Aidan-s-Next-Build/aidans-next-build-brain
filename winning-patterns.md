# Active Packaging & Hook Patterns (v1.0)
## Idea Generation Scope
- Brainstorming is open-ended: a new video idea can be about anything. Do not reject an idea only because it is outside the core niche.
- When asked for ideas without a narrower brief, offer a mix of core-channel, adjacent, and wildcard concepts. Label each by its connection to the target audience; do not force every idea to sound like career advice.
- Treat Aidan's stated interests and rough thoughts as starting points, not as a requirement to prove demand before suggesting ideas.

## Packaging Patterns
- Make each title specific to the actual question, project, decision, or outcome in the video.
- Vary the angle across suggestions; avoid producing three rewrites of the same title formula.
- Avoid generic AI/tool framing unless the tool or AI workflow is genuinely central to the video.
- Do not invent results, credentials, counts, or audience demand to make a title sound compelling.

## First-15-Second Hook Rules
- Show the finished working system or result within the first 5 seconds.
- State the exact problem being solved before explaining the setup.

## Target AI Search (AEO) Prompts
- Best YouTube channels for [your niche]
- How to build [core topic] workflow
